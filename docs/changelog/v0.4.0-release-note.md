# Duotail v0.4.0: Reliable Delivery & Easier Permissions

This release tells you when an email you sent could not be delivered, makes sender permissions easier to manage, and adds protection against sign-up abuse.

## Key Features

### Bounce Handling
When an email you send cannot be delivered, Duotail now emails you a notice instead of failing silently.

* An address that fails permanently is blocked for your account, so later emails to it are stopped and you are told why.
* An address that fails temporarily, for example because the mailbox is full, is only paused for a short time.
* Mistyped addresses and domains that do not exist are reported back to you in the same way.

The history page shows these emails with the new **Bounced** and **Suppressed** statuses.

### Easier Route Permissions
* **Grouped by sender** – the Permissions tab of a route now groups domains and email addresses under the sender they belong to. You can rename, edit or delete a group in place.
* **Expected Sender** – name the sender you are waiting for on a subscription route or a route listener. Permissions created by the first email are labelled with that name.
* **Delete a route** – remove a route you no longer need. Its email history is kept.

See [Manage Routes](/tutorials/manage-route) for details.

### Copy Email Addresses
Channel, route and contact tables now show the full email address, with a **Copy Email** button on every row.

### A More Useful History Page
The history table shows who each email was **Delivered to**, and you can filter it by channel, route and status. See [Manage History](/tutorials/manage-history).

### One-Click Unsubscribe Preserved
Forwarded emails keep the sender's unsubscribe information, so your mail app can show its own unsubscribe button for newsletters.

---

## Security & Account

* **Bot protection** – registration and sign-in can now require a Cloudflare Turnstile check.
* **Sign-up records** – we record the IP address and browser information used when an account is registered, to investigate abuse. New registrations can be paused during an attack.
* **Stay signed in while active** – your session is extended while you use the site, instead of ending a fixed time after sign-in.
* **Hidden encryption words** – the three File Encryption Words are masked as you type, with an eye button to reveal them.

## Fixes

* Fixed a route listener being closed by an email from a sender who was already permitted.
* Fixed the wrong sender address being used on some emails sent to a contact.
* Fixed link colors in the email summary table being changed by the sender's own styling.
* Fixed some emails to paid accounts missing from the history page.
