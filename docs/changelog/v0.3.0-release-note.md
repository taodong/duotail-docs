# Duotail v0.3.0: Total Sovereignty & Zero-Knowledge Security

This milestone release transforms Duotail from a privacy gateway into a full-scale **Zero-Knowledge vault**. We have introduced mathematical safeguards that ensure your data remains yours—and only yours.

## 🛡️ Key Features

### Zero-Knowledge Email Encryption
Your privacy is now backed by industry-standard cryptography. Using **AES-256** and **Argon2id**, your incoming emails are encrypted the moment they hit our gateway. 
* **The "Three Words" Secret:** We don't store your keys. Your messages are decrypted in your browser’s transient memory using your unique secret words.
* **Benefit:** Even if our servers were compromised, your data remains indistinguishable from random noise.

### The Route Listener (Smart Automation)
Stop the manual back-and-forth of whitelisting. The new **Route Listener** allows you to open a specific "airlock" for a set duration (30m, 1h, or 8h).
* **How it works:** It catches the next incoming email, automatically whitelists the sender, and then snaps shut.
* **Perfect for:** Quick sign-ups and newsletter subscriptions without leaving your inbox exposed.

### One-Click Data Portability
True privacy requires an exit strategy. You can now download your entire configuration — channel, routes, contacts, and permission settings — in a single, portable JSON file. Your setup belongs to you, not our database.

### Instant Account Deactivation
We believe in the "Right to be Forgotten." If you choose to leave, our new one-click deactivation ensures all your records and configurations are purged from our infrastructure immediately.

We’ve polished the interface to ensure your "Inbox Zen" follows you across all devices:

* **iOS Dark Mode Fix:** Resolved a display issue where the **Email Summary** columns were unreadable in dark mode.
* **Mobile Responsiveness:** Fixed table overflow issues on mobile browsers; managing your routes on the go is now a seamless experience.
* **Route Table Logic:** Improved visibility for Subscription Route statuses and listener indicators.

> **Note on Encryption:** Because Duotail is a zero-knowledge system, we do not have a "backdoor." If you lose your secret words, encrypted messages become permanently inaccessible. We prioritize your security over our convenience.
