# Cookie Disclosure

At Duotail, we believe in radical transparency. Most websites hide hundreds of tracking pixels behind vague "Policy" pages. We prefer a different approach: **an explicit, exhaustive list of every cookie we use.**

If it isn't on this list, it isn't on our platform.

---

## 🍪 Infrastructure Summary

Our commitment to **Data Minimalism** extends to your browser. 
- We do not use advertising cookies.
- We do not use cross-site tracking pixels.
- We do not use third-party analytics cookies.

Our cookies are strictly **Essential**—they exist solely to maintain your secure session and ensure the gateway functions correctly.

## 🛡️ Your Privacy Sovereignty

Because we do not use non-essential or tracking cookies, you will not see an intrusive "Cookie Banner" on our site. We don't need your permission to track you because **we don't track you.**

If you have any questions regarding our browser-side persistence, please refer to our [Privacy & Security Summary](/privacy).
