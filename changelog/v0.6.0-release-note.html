Duotail v0.6.0: Group Emails & Promotion Codes

This release lets you email several contacts at once and reply all to group emails, adds promotion codes, and gives you a better way to stop senders that keep changing their address.

Key Features​

Email Several Contacts at Once (Paid Plans)​

Put any number of 2_ contact addresses in To, Cc and Bcc, up to 50 per email. Everyone receives the same message from your Duotail address, and contacts in To and Cc can reply all.

  • If some of the addresses don't have a contact yet, the email waits in Send Pending. Clicking Send walks you through creating each missing contact, and contacts you already created are kept if you stop part way.
  • Each recipient counts as one email toward your monthly sending limit.
  • The email is shown as one row on the History page.

See Sending to Several Contacts.

Reply All on Emails You Receive​

When someone emails you and copies other people, your copy now shows those people under Duotail 3_ addresses. Reply All in your mail app sends your reply to everyone through Duotail, so your real address stays private. The email summary warns you when some of them aren't contacts of the channel. See Reply All on Received Emails.

Upgrade with a Promotion Code​

Have a promotion code? Click Promo Code in the top menu bar of your Dashboard, check the code and confirm. Your account is upgraded to the Basic plan for the number of months the code grants, with no payment needed. Each code can be used once per account. See Upgrade with a Promotion Code.

You can now also upgrade during the short grace period after a paid plan ends.

Stop Senders That Keep Changing Their Address​

Some senders use a new address for every email, so a One Shot route lets each one through. The email summary of a One Shot route now offers Stop receiving email from domain, which blocks every later email from that sender's exact domain on that route. See Email Summary.

Improvements & Fixes​

  • Basic plan channel limits: a Basic plan includes up to 1 identity channel and 3 alias channels. The Add Inbox window tells you when a limit is reached. Channels you already own are kept. See Channel Limits by Plan.
  • Field help on the register and account pages: Key Name and File Encryption Words now have a ? icon that explains the field.
  • Clearer registration message: the success screen now shows your main email address and points you to Create Email Address for more.
  • Fixed undeliverable-email notices not being received for emails you sent.
