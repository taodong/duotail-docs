Duotail v0.7.0: Account Deletion & Verified Senders

This release lets you delete your account yourself, makes sure every sender you see is genuine, and shows who an email is from right in your mail app.

Key Features​

Delete Your Account​

You can now permanently delete your account. Click Delete Account on the Account page, or open the public Delete Account page, which works without signing in. Enter your username and password, confirm that you understand it can't be undone, and your account is deleted straight away.

  • Your channels, routes, contacts and email history records are removed, and all your Duotail addresses stop working.
  • A paid subscription is cancelled immediately. Payments already made are not refunded.
  • We send a confirmation email to your primary email address.

Delete Account replaces the former Deactivate Account button. See Deleting Your Account.

Verified Senders Only​

Duotail now delivers an incoming email only when the domain in its From field is verified by DMARC (through SPF or DKIM). Emails with a forged sender are deleted before they reach you. See Sender Verification.

See the Real Sender in Your Mail App​

Emails we deliver come from your masked Duotail address, so your mail app couldn't show who actually sent them. The From display name now shows the original sender, for example Harry Potter harry3432.._at_gmail.com. See The Sender in Your Inbox.

Encrypted-Mail Senders Delivered Untouched​

Emails from encrypted-mail providers such as Proton are now delivered exactly as sent, without a summary table, so their signatures and encryption keep working. See Emails Without a Summary.

Alias Notice for Your Contacts​

Emails you send to a contact from an alias channel now carry a short notice telling the recipient that the sender name was chosen by the alias owner. See Alias Notice.

Improvements & Fixes​

  • No Duotail addresses as your email: you can no longer register with, or add, an address on duotail.com as your own email, because mail forwarded to it would loop back into Duotail.
  • History page: Blocked and Suppressed rows no longer show Download and Delete buttons, since those records have no stored file.
