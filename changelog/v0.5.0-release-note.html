Duotail v0.5.0: A Dashboard for Everyday Email

This release puts the things you do most often on one page, lets you upgrade to a paid plan online, and gives you more control over emails that were held back.

Key Features​

The New Dashboard​

The home page is now a single Dashboard. You no longer need to open an inbox to do the basics:

  • Create Email Address – a short step-by-step window that gives you a new address to hand out.
  • Allow Incoming Emails – open an address to one new sender for 30 minutes, 1 hour or 8 hours.
  • Add Inbox – set up another inbox with its own main email (paid plans).
  • Activity – see which addresses are listening right now and your five latest emails.

New to Duotail? The Quick Start guide walks through the whole page.

Upgrade Online​

You can now upgrade to the Basic plan and pay by card through Stripe, without contacting us. You can also cancel by yourself; your plan stays active until the end of the period you already paid for.

Send First, Add the Contact Later (Paid Plans)​

Write to a new 2_<name>@duotail.com address straight from your mail app. Duotail holds the email in the new Send Pending section of the dashboard until you tell it who the address belongs to, then sends it. See Send Email.

Paid plans now include a monthly number of emails you can start this way. When you reach it, further emails wait in Send Pending until the next month.

Read and Release Emails from History​

  • View – read a Sent or Saved email in your browser after entering your encryption words. Images and anything that could track you or run code are removed first.
  • Send – deliver a Saved email to your own inbox if you decide you want it after all.
  • The channel and route filters now also cover blocked, saved, suppressed and failed emails.

Clearer Warnings About Senders​

  • The email summary table shows a red warning when the sender is one you have not heard from before.
  • A new [Public Email Service] alert marks senders who use a shared provider such as Gmail or Yahoo.
  • When such a sender is permitted, only that exact email address is allowed, not everyone at the same provider.

Improvements & Fixes​

  • The email summary table is no longer included when you print an email or save it as a PDF.
  • The allow and block shortcuts in the summary table now behave the same way for both types of email address.
  • Fixed the Expected Sender name not being saved when an email arrived through Allow Incoming Emails.
  • Fixed Allow next message failing for some sender addresses that contain capital letters.
  • Fixed the sign-in page sometimes asking you to complete a verification check that was not shown yet.
  • Fixed the close button overlapping the title in some pop-up windows.
